## Project overview

This repository presents the end-to-end Business Intelligence solution developed as my **Master's Thesis in Business Intelligence and Data Analytics**.

The project transforms multiple operational and external data sources into interactive dashboards, analytical models and actionable business insights. 

It covers the complete Business Intelligence process, including data validation, data transformation, exploratory analysis, data modelling, dashboard development, outlet segmentation and forecasting.

## Project methodology

The project follows an end-to-end Business Intelligence workflow:

1. Collection and integration of operational and external data sources
2. Data validation and exploratory analysis using R
3. Data cleaning and transformation using Power Query
4. Data modelling and relationship configuration in Power BI
5. Creation of DAX measures and business KPIs
6. Development of interactive dashboards
7. Outlet segmentation and clustering analysis
8. Sales trend analysis and forecasting
9. Interpretation of results and development of business recommendations
